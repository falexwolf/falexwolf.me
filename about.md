Title: About

I live in Munich with my wife Sunny and three kids. We also regularly spend time with family, friends, and colleagues in the US and in China.

I am the co-founder and CEO of [Lamin](https://lamin.ai). You can read up on what we're doing there on [lamin.ai/about](https://lamin.ai/about) and on [docs.lamin.ai](https://docs.lamin.ai). Consider dropping me a message via [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/falexwolf) if you think that's interesting!

I'm trained as an academic researcher and so I have an [academic CV](/CV.pdf) and a Google Scholar [profile](http://scholar.google.de/citations?user=1FnOtMoAAAAJ). Misc things that you don't find on the CV:

* I love the work.
* I'm grateful to have found an interesting topic to work on after a long journey through different areas of interest since childhood. I'm grateful to be working alongside my wife.
* I enjoy playing all kinds of sports with the kids and to go running & hiking. As a teen I competed in running, cycling, Judo & Jujutsu.
* I've enjoyed living & working in different places, as a scientist and engineer and in my teens as a social worker & kitchen aid: Rosenheim, Ribeauville, Dehradun, Augsburg, DC, Paris, Stuttgart, NYC, Boston.
